I have been making pottery at Homer Watson House & Gallery since April 2025. I can work safely and independently in the Coach House: centre my own clay, throw and trim, hand-build to an even thickness, glaze my own pots, and see pieces through drying and firing.

On the wheel I throw cups, bowls, plates, vases, mugs, a pour-over set, a pitcher, and lidded jars. I trim feet, pull handles, and fit lids. Off the wheel I pinch, coil, and slab-build, and I add carved or sculptural details (wheat-stalk relief, folded rims, leaf attachments, a frog salt cellar). I glaze my own work with the studio glazes.

The attached portfolio is recent finished ware, 2025–2026. I completed Open Studio in winter 2026 and am applying to continue working independently.
